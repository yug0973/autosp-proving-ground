How autosp closes a finding.

Keep your scanner. autosp takes what it reports and hands back a fix with proof.

  1. 01FindIt reads the report from the scanner you already run.
  2. 02ReproduceThe exploit becomes a test. It must fail first.
  3. 03FixThe smallest patch, in a sealed copy of your code.
  4. 04VerifyThree checks the agents can't see or change.
  5. 05ReviewA pull request with the proof. You decide.

What lands in your repo

  • A failing testThe exploit, written as a test that fails before the fix and passes after.
  • The smallest patchOne focused diff, made in a sealed copy of your code.
  • Three gate resultsExploit test, full suite, fresh scan. Run by a verifier the agents can't touch.
  • An audit trailEvery step logged, each entry chained to the last.
